ATLASLE

guess the country in eight tries
How to play ▾

A secret country has been chosen. Type any country and submit. Each guess reveals eight clues comparing your guess to the secret country. Colors tell you how close you are.

Green = exact match Amber = close Red = far off

The eight clues

Important: Size, Population, and Economy show the difference between your guess and the secret country — not your guess's own stats. "39k mi² off ↓ smaller" means the secret country is 39k mi² smaller than what you guessed, not that your guess is 39k mi².

ContinentGreen if same continent, red if not.
LetterFirst letter of the country's official name. Green if it matches.
SizeLand area. Shows how many square miles the secret country is off from your guess, plus an arrow: ↑ the secret country is bigger, ↓ it's smaller.
PopulationHow many people the secret country is off from your guess, with ↑ (secret has more) or ↓ (fewer).
EconomyGDP. How far off the secret country is in US dollars, with ↑ (secret is richer) or ↓ (poorer).
GovernmentType of government (republic, constitutional monarchy, etc.). Green if it's the same.
LanguageMost widely spoken language. Green if it's the same.
FlagPercent of the secret flag's colors (including emblems and coats of arms) that also appear on your guess's flag. Guess USA against China → 50% (both have red).

Tip: the size and economy arrows are your compass — follow them toward bigger/smaller and richer/poorer countries.
