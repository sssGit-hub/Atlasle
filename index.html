guess the country in six tries
How to play ▾

A secret country has been chosen. Type any country and submit. Each guess reveals eight clues comparing your guess to the secret country. Colors tell you how close you are.

Green = exact match Amber = close Red = far off

The eight clues

ContinentGreen if same continent, red if not.
LetterFirst letter of the country's official name. Green if it matches.
SizeLand area. Shows how many square miles your guess is off, plus an arrow: ↑ the secret country is bigger, ↓ it's smaller.
PopulationShows how many people off your guess is, with ↑ (secret has more) or ↓ (fewer).
EconomyGDP. Shows how far off in US dollars, with ↑ (secret is richer) or ↓ (poorer).
GovernmentType of government (republic, constitutional monarchy, etc.). Green if it's the same.
LanguageMost widely spoken language. Green if it's the same.
FlagPercent of the secret flag's colors that also appear on your guess's flag. Guess USA against China → 50% (both have red).

Tip: the size and economy arrows are your compass — follow them toward bigger/smaller and richer/poorer countries.
